How to Use MarketingTool: Your Complete Getting Started Guide

Get Up and Running With AI Marketing Tools in Minutes

MarketingTool brings together specialized AI tools across Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and Website optimization into one unified platform. Whether you are running your first campaign or managing enterprise-level accounts, this guide walks you through everything you need to know to start generating results immediately. Every feature has been designed for marketers who need actionable output, not just data.

Step 1: Create Your Account and Start Your Free Trial

Visit app.marketingtool.pro and register using your email address, Google account, or Facebook login. The moment you sign up, your 7-day free trial begins with full access to all tools across every platform category. No credit card is required during the trial period, and you can explore every feature without restrictions. Your account dashboard will guide you through initial setup with a personalized onboarding checklist.

  • Register at app.marketingtool.pro with email, Google, or Facebook
  • 7-day free trial activates immediately with all tools unlocked
  • No credit card needed to begin exploring the platform
  • Onboarding checklist guides you through first-time setup tasks

Step 2: Connect Your Advertising and Analytics Accounts

To unlock the full power of MarketingTool, connect your existing ad accounts and analytics platforms. The integration process uses secure OAuth authentication, meaning your credentials are never stored on our servers. Once connected, the AI agents immediately begin analyzing your historical campaign data to generate baseline performance metrics and identify the highest-impact optimization opportunities.

  • Connect Google Ads, Facebook Business Manager, and Google Analytics with one-click OAuth
  • Shopify and WooCommerce store connections for e-commerce tools
  • Historical data imports start within seconds of connection
  • Secure OAuth means your login credentials never touch our servers

Step 3: Choose Your Tools by Platform Category

MarketingTool organizes its tools into three main platform categories. Google tools cover ads optimization, keyword research, campaign management, and performance grading. Facebook tools handle ad creation, audience targeting, creative generation, and Instagram marketing. Website and Shopify tools address SEO, content writing, analytics, site auditing, and e-commerce optimization. Navigate to any category from the dashboard sidebar to see every available tool with descriptions of what each one does.

  • Google platform: 56 tools for ads, optimization, and campaign management
  • Facebook platform: 61 tools for Meta, Instagram, AI agents, and e-commerce
  • Website and Shopify platform: 77 tools for content, SEO, analytics, and graders
  • Each tool includes a detailed description and example output preview

Step 4: Generate Content and Run AI Analysis

Select any tool and provide the required inputs. For content generation tools, enter your product details, target audience, and preferred tone. For analysis tools, select the account or campaign you want to evaluate. The AI processes your request using advanced language models trained specifically on marketing data, delivering professional-grade output in seconds. Every generation can be edited, regenerated with different parameters, or exported directly to your workflow.

  • Fill in the tool-specific input fields with your campaign details
  • AI generates professional marketing output in seconds
  • Edit, regenerate, or export results directly from the tool interface
  • Each tool tracks your generation history for easy reference

Step 5: Review Results and Optimize Your Campaigns

After generating content or running an analysis, review the output carefully and apply it to your campaigns. The platform provides contextual tips alongside every result to help you understand why specific recommendations were made. Use the built-in A/B testing suggestions to compare different approaches and track which variations perform best across your campaigns over time.

  • Contextual tips explain the reasoning behind every recommendation
  • A/B testing suggestions help you compare different creative approaches
  • Performance tracking shows which AI-generated content converts best
  • Continuous optimization cycle improves results with every iteration

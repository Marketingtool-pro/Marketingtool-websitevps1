Campaign Structure and Organization Audit

The foundation of any successful advertising account is its structure. Our audit examines how your campaigns, ad groups, and ad sets are organized to identify fragmentation, overlap, and naming inconsistencies that make management difficult and optimization impossible. We evaluate whether your account follows platform best practices for campaign hierarchy, budget allocation, and audience segmentation.   • Campaign hierarchy analysis to detect fragmentation and overlap

The audit reviews your keyword lists for search campaigns, identifying low Quality Score terms, broad match waste, missing negative keywords, and underperforming search queries that drain your budget. For social campaigns, we analyze your audience definitions, lookalike quality, exclusion lists, and interest targeting to find opportunities for tighter, more profitable audience segments that reduce cost per acquisition.

  • Quality Score breakdown for every keyword in your Google Ads account

AI Google Ads Audit: Common Questions

What is an AI Google Ads audit?

An AI Google Ads audit reads your account data (campaigns, keywords, search terms, ads, budgets and conversion tracking) and lists the problems in priority order: spend on search terms that never convert, campaigns limited by budget, weak ads and gaps in conversion tracking.

How often should I audit a Google Ads account?

Run a full audit before you raise budgets or change bidding strategy, and at least once a quarter. Review search terms and budget pacing every week, because wasted spend builds up fastest there.

What should I fix first after a Google Ads audit?

Start with conversion tracking, because if conversions are counted wrong every other number is wrong. Then add negative keywords for wasted search terms, move budget to campaigns that are limited by budget but hit your CPA target, and only then test new ad copy.

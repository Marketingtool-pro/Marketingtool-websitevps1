Payment Security

We take payment security seriously. Your financial data is handled with the highest industry standards.

We do NOT store your payment card data on our servers

All transactions are processed by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway — the highest level of certification in the payment card industry. Stripe processes billions of dollars annually for companies like Google, Amazon, and Shopify.

  • PCI DSS Level 1 Compliant: All card data is handled exclusively by Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure. We never see, store, or process your full card number, CVV, or expiration date.
  • TLS 1.2+ Encryption: All data in transit between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher, ensuring no third party can intercept your information.
  • Tokenized Payments: Stripe replaces your card details with a secure token. Even if our systems were breached, no card data could be exposed because it simply doesn't exist on our servers.
  • 3D Secure Authentication: For supported cards, we use 3D Secure (Verified by Visa, Mastercard SecureCode) for an additional layer of authentication.
  • No Shared Data: We do not sell, share, or transfer your payment information to any third party beyond the payment processor (Stripe).

Security & Infrastructure

Our platform is built with enterprise-grade security measures to protect your data and advertising accounts.

Authentication & Access
  • OAuth 2.0 for Google and Meta account connections
  • Secure JWT-based session management
  • Passwords hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
  • Session timeout and automatic token expiry
  • Rate limiting on all authentication endpoints
Data Protection
  • TLS 1.2+ encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) on all 27 database tables
  • No direct database access from frontend — all queries go through authenticated backend
  • Regular automated backups with point-in-time recovery
Infrastructure
  • HTTPS enforced on all endpoints (HSTS enabled)
  • Firewall-protected servers with restricted access
  • Automated health checks and recovery (every 2 minutes)
  • Daily automated backups at 2 AM and 3 AM
  • Self-hosted analytics (Matomo) — no third-party trackers
API Security
  • All API endpoints require JWT authentication
  • Google Ads API: Limited Use compliance (read-only analytics, no data sharing)
  • Meta Marketing API: Compliant with Meta Platform Terms
  • API keys and secrets stored in encrypted environment variables — never in code
  • Ad account tokens stored securely with automatic refresh

Refund Policy

We want you to be satisfied with our service. If you're not, here's how refunds work:

Monthly Subscriptions
  • You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time from your account settings.
  • Upon cancellation, you retain access until the end of your current billing period.
  • No partial-month refunds are issued for monthly plans.
  • No refund is given if the subscription has been actively used during the billing cycle.
Annual Subscriptions
  • Annual plans may be eligible for a pro-rated refund within 30 days of purchase if fewer than 50 AI generations have been used.
  • After 30 days or if significant usage has occurred, no refund will be issued.
  • Refund amount = (unused months / 12) x annual price paid.
Extra Token Purchases
  • Token packs ($3 per 100 generations) are non-refundable once purchased.
  • Unused tokens remain in your account and do not expire while your account is active.
How to Request a Refund

Email help@marketingtool.pro with your account email and reason for the refund. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days back to your original payment method via Stripe.

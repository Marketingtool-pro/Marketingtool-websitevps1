AI Marketing Agents

What are AI Agents?

AI Marketing Agents are autonomous systems that continuously monitor and optimize your campaigns 24/7 without manual intervention. They learn from your data and make intelligent decisions to improve performance.

How They Work
  • Connect your ad accounts to enable AI access
  • Set your goals (CPA, ROAS, conversions)
  • Enable agents and let them optimize automatically
  • Review AI recommendations and performance reports

Available Agents

Agent Types
  • Bid Optimizer: Automatically adjusts bids based on real-time performance data and competition
  • Budget Allocator: Distributes budget across campaigns and ad sets for maximum ROI
  • Keyword Manager: Discovers, adds, pauses, and optimizes keywords based on performance
  • Ad Scheduler: Optimizes ad delivery times based on when your audience converts
  • Creative Tester: Runs A/B tests and identifies winning creatives

Integrations

Supported Platforms

  • Google Ads: Full campaign management and optimization
  • Facebook/Meta Ads: Ad management, audience insights, and pixel tracking
  • Instagram: Content scheduling, story ads, and analytics
  • Shopify: E-commerce tracking, product feeds, and dynamic ads
  • Google Analytics 4: Website performance and conversion tracking
  • Microsoft Ads: Bing campaign management

Go to Settings > Integrations and click "Connect" next to each platform. Follow the OAuth flow to authorize MarketingTool.

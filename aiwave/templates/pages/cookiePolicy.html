Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 6, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how MarketingTool.pro ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website and use our services. By using our platform, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Keep you securely logged in
  • Understand how you use our platform
  • Improve your user experience
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are required for the platform to function properly:

  • Session Cookies: Keep you logged in during your session (Appwrite authentication).
  • CSRF Tokens: Protect against cross-site request forgery attacks.
  • Security Cookies: Protect against fraudulent activity and ensure secure connections.
  • Load Balancing: Distribute server load for optimal performance.

You cannot disable these cookies as they are essential for the Service to work.

2.2 Functional Cookies (Preference Cookies)

These cookies remember your choices and preferences:

  • Language and region preferences
  • Theme and display settings
  • Dashboard layout customization
  • Recently used tools and favorites
  • Sidebar state and navigation preferences
2.3 Analytics Cookies (Performance Cookies)

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our platform:

  • Matomo Analytics (Self-Hosted): Track page views, session duration, and user behavior. Unlike third-party analytics, Matomo runs on our own servers, so your data stays with us and is not shared with external parties.

The information collected is aggregated and used to improve our platform and user experience.

2.4 Authentication Cookies (Third-Party Login)

When you sign in using third-party providers, the following cookies may be set:

  • Google OAuth: Authentication cookies set by Google during the OAuth 2.0 sign-in flow. These are governed by Google's Cookie Policy.
  • Facebook Login: Authentication cookies set by Meta during the Facebook Login flow. These are governed by Meta's Cookie Policy.

These cookies are only set when you actively choose to sign in with these providers.

2.5 Payment Cookies

When you make a payment, our payment processor sets cookies:

  • Stripe: Fraud prevention and payment processing cookies. These are governed by Stripe's Cookie Policy.

4. Google and Meta Advertising Cookies

When you connect your Google Ads or Meta advertising accounts to our platform, the following may apply:

Google Ads Integration

Google may set cookies during the OAuth authentication process. These cookies are managed by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control Google's cookies. Our platform accesses your Google Ads data through API calls, not through cookies.

Meta (Facebook) Integration

Meta may set cookies during the Facebook Login process. These cookies are managed by Meta and are subject to Meta's Privacy Policy. We do not control Meta's cookies. Our platform accesses your Meta advertising data through API calls, not through cookies.

Important: We do not use Google or Meta cookies to track you across the web, serve you advertisements, or build advertising profiles. These cookies are only used for the authentication flow when you connect your ad accounts.

5. How Long Do Cookies Last?

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device until they expire or you delete them. Our persistent cookies last from 30 minutes to 13 months, depending on their purpose.

6. How to Manage and Control Cookies

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies
Third-Party Opt-Out
  • Google: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Google: Google Ads Settings
  • Meta: Facebook Ad Preferences
  • DAA: Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out
  • NAI: Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out

Important: Blocking or deleting essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using certain features of our platform.

7. Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Our self-hosted Matomo analytics respects the Do Not Track browser setting. If your browser sends a DNT signal, Matomo will not track your visit. For other cookies, we provide the management options described above.

8. Mobile Devices and Apps

When you access our platform through mobile devices or our mobile apps, we may use similar technologies:

  • Mobile device identifiers for authentication
  • Local storage for session management and preferences
  • SDK-based analytics (Matomo SDK)

You can manage these through your device settings or app permissions.

9. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top and, for material changes, by posting a notice on our platform or sending an email.

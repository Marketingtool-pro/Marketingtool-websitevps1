Multi-Platform Campaign Hub: Manage Every Channel from One Place

Control Google, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and More from a Single Unified Interface

Running campaigns across multiple advertising platforms means juggling different interfaces, metrics, terminologies, and optimization levers. Your team wastes hours switching between Google Ads, Facebook Business Manager, TikTok Ads Manager, and LinkedIn Campaign Manager just to get a basic understanding of overall performance. Our Multi-Platform Campaign Hub eliminates that fragmentation by bringing every channel into one unified interface. Create campaigns, manage budgets, monitor performance, and optimize across all platforms without ever leaving a single dashboard. The result is faster execution, better cross-channel coordination, and a complete view of your marketing ecosystem.

Unified Campaign Creation and Cross-Platform Launch

Launching a campaign across multiple platforms traditionally requires building it separately in each platform's interface, adapting creative specs, adjusting targeting parameters, and setting individual budgets. Our unified campaign creator lets you build once and deploy everywhere. Define your audience, upload your creative assets, set your budget, and launch across Google, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn simultaneously. The system automatically adapts your campaign settings to each platform's requirements while maintaining strategic consistency.

  • Build campaigns once using a unified interface and deploy to Google, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn simultaneously
  • Automatic creative adaptation that resizes and reformats assets to meet each platform's exact specifications
  • Cross-platform audience mapping that translates your targeting criteria into each platform's native targeting parameters
  • Synchronized launch scheduling that coordinates campaign start times across all platforms for maximum impact

Centralized Budget Distribution and Spend Control

Managing budgets across platforms is one of the most complex challenges in multi-channel marketing. Each platform has its own budget models, pacing algorithms, and spending patterns. Our centralized budget management system lets you set a total marketing budget and intelligently distribute it across platforms based on performance data. The AI-powered allocation engine continuously monitors cross-platform ROI and shifts spend toward the channels delivering the best results, ensuring every dollar works as hard as possible.

  • Set a total marketing budget and let AI distribute spend across platforms based on real-time performance data
  • Real-time spend tracking across all platforms with unified currency conversion and consolidated cost reporting
  • Automatic budget rebalancing that shifts spend from underperforming channels to high-ROI platforms within hours
  • Budget alerts and guardrails that prevent overspending on any single platform while maximizing total portfolio return

Cross-Channel Performance Comparison and Insights

Comparing performance across platforms is notoriously difficult because each one defines metrics differently. A Facebook impression is not the same as a Google Display impression, and TikTok engagement metrics have no direct equivalent on LinkedIn. Our cross-channel comparison engine normalizes metrics across all platforms so you can make apples-to-apples comparisons. See which platform delivers the lowest cost per acquisition, highest return on ad spend, and best audience quality, all in standardized views that make decision-making straightforward.

  • Normalized metrics that standardize impressions, clicks, conversions, and engagement across all connected platforms
  • Side-by-side platform comparison dashboards showing CPA, ROAS, CTR, and conversion rates on equal footing
  • Cross-channel audience overlap analysis that identifies where the same users are being targeted on multiple platforms
  • AI-generated insights that recommend optimal platform mix and budget allocation based on historical cross-channel data

What You Get

  • Unified campaign creation interface that deploys to Google, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn simultaneously
  • Centralized budget management with AI-powered cross-platform allocation and real-time spend tracking
  • Normalized cross-channel metrics for accurate platform comparison on CPA, ROAS, CTR, and conversion rate
  • Automatic creative adaptation that reformats assets for each platform's specific requirements and placements
  • Cross-channel audience overlap detection to eliminate wasted spend from targeting the same users across platforms
  • AI-generated platform mix recommendations based on historical performance data and real-time market conditions

What a PPC audit checks

A PPC audit is a structured review of a pay-per-click account that shows where budget is being lost and which fixes matter most. The AI PPC audit connects to your Google Ads or Meta Ads account and reviews the same areas an experienced PPC manager checks by hand:

  • Account structure – campaigns and ad groups that mix unrelated themes, overlapping audiences, and duplicate keywords that compete with each other.
  • Wasted spend – search terms, placements and audiences that spend money without converting, and negative keywords that are missing.
  • Keywords and match types – broad match terms without guardrails, low Quality Score keywords and keywords that never get impressions.
  • Bids and budgets – campaigns limited by budget while others underspend, and bid strategies that do not fit your conversion volume.
  • Ads and landing pages – ad groups with too few ads, weak headlines, and landing pages that do not match the search intent.
  • Conversion tracking – missing or duplicated conversion actions, which make every other number in the account unreliable.

How to do a PPC audit, step by step

  1. Confirm conversion tracking is correct before you judge any campaign.
  2. Pull the last 30–90 days of search terms and placements and mark what is irrelevant.
  3. Compare cost per conversion by campaign, ad group and keyword, and list the biggest spenders with the weakest results.
  4. Check budgets and bid strategies against your target CPA or ROAS.
  5. Review the ads and landing pages behind your top-spending ad groups.
  6. Turn the findings into a prioritized fix list, apply it, and re-check results after two to four weeks.

Manual PPC audit vs. an AI PPC audit tool

A manual PPC account audit takes a specialist many hours, so most teams only do it once a quarter. An AI PPC audit tool reads the same data in minutes, which means you can audit every month or after every major change. The result is a PPC audit report with each issue ranked by its likely impact on spend, so the expensive problems get fixed first.
